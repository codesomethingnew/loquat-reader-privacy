Loquat Reader Support

Help with feeds, reading, and writing in Loquat Reader.

Adding RSS feeds

Use the app's add-feed option to enter a feed address. If you don't know the RSS or Atom address, try entering the publisher's website address so Loquat Reader can look for a feed. Some websites do not provide a compatible feed.

Importing and exporting OPML

OPML files let you move a list of feed subscriptions between reader apps. Use Loquat Reader's import option to choose an OPML file from your device. To make a copy of your subscriptions for use elsewhere, use the app's OPML export option and choose where to save or share the file.

Saving articles

Use an article's save or bookmark control to keep it for later. Find saved articles in the app's saved list. You can remove an article from that list using its save control again.

Loquat Write

Loquat Write is for creating and keeping your own documents in the app. Your writing is stored locally on your device, so consider exporting or sharing a copy if you want a separate backup.

Reporting a bug

Email loquatreader@gmail.com with a short description of the problem. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, the steps to reproduce the issue, and what you expected to happen. Please leave out passwords and other sensitive information.
