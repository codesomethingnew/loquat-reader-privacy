Loquat Reader Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Loquat Reader ("we") respects your privacy. This policy describes how the Loquat Reader mobile app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Loquat Reader does not require an account and does not operate a Loquat cloud service for syncing user data. The app stores information locally on your device, including feed subscriptions and folders, saved articles, reading status, appearance and reading preferences, article notes and highlights, and documents created in Write.

This information remains in the app's local storage until removed using available app controls or the app is removed. There is no single in-app control to erase all app data. Device backup and retention behavior may depend on your operating-system settings.

Information shared with other services

The app contacts third-party services to provide features you use:

  • RSS publishers and websites, to retrieve feeds and discover feeds when you add a website.
  • Arquivo.pt and the Internet Archive, when you search their archives. Search terms are included in those requests.
  • rssamplifier.com, when you use online publication search. Your search text is included in that request.
  • Google services, to load feed favicon images and web fonts in article reading. Favicon requests include the relevant website domain.
  • Websites that host images displayed from feed content.

Those services receive the requests needed to return content and may process information associated with network requests, such as an IP address, under their own policies. Loquat Reader does not control those services' data practices. The app does not send locally stored notes or writing to a Loquat-operated server.

Files, sharing, and speech

When you import subscriptions, the app uses the system document picker to access the file you select. When you export or share content, the system share interface lets you choose a destination. Article narration uses the device's speech facilities; processing may depend on the operating system and selected voice.

Advertising and analytics

The current app does not integrate Google AdMob or another advertising SDK, and does not currently display ads. AdMob may be considered for a future version; it is not part of the current app. The current project dependencies and code reviewed do not show an analytics SDK. If these practices change, this policy and applicable app privacy disclosures will be updated.

Your choices and data removal

You can remove feeds, saved articles, notes, and writing using the available app controls. The app does not have an account or central Loquat data store for account deletion. Information already sent to third-party services is subject to those services' policies; contact them directly about their records.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions, contact Loquat Reader at loquatreader@gmail.com. We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The effective date above will reflect the publication date of the current policy.
